# Software Lead – Western Aero Design

Architecting an end-to-end **autonomous mission system** for a VTOL platform, defining GNC workflows, mission sequencing, and service boundaries across flight and payload subsystems.

## Key Responsibilities

- **Autonomous Mission System**: Architected an end-to-end autonomous mission system for a VTOL platform, defining **GNC workflows**, mission sequencing, and service boundaries across flight and payload subsystems.
- **PX4 Firmware**: Developed and customized **PX4 firmware**, including custom drivers and bootloader modifications, enabling seamless integration of onboard sensors, payload systems, and communication interfaces.
- **Telemetry & Command Protocols**: Designed and implemented low-latency, secure telemetry and command protocols (custom message schemas over **LoRa**), enabling real-time coordination between aircraft, payload, and ground station under constrained network conditions.
- **Payload System**: Engineered a **Raspberry Pi–based payload system**, integrating motors, sensors, and embedded Linux services; delivered fault-tolerant autonomous deployment with reliable hardware–software interfacing (**SPI/I2C/UART**).
- **SIL/HIL Testing**: Led System Integration (SIL) and Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) testing pipelines, driving iterative debugging, parameter tuning, and root-cause analysis to reduce mission-critical failures.
- **Competition Results**: 🏆 Achieved **3rd place in Presentation** and **4th place in Technical Report** at **SAE Aero Design 2026** (Tampa, Florida).
